# Submission checklist: CEDA Innovation Management Portal Hackathon

Deadline: **Monday 23 November 2026, 17:00 (Botswana time)**. Total upload: **150 MB at most**.

## Mandatory

- [ ] **Live URL:** https://ceda-innovation-portal.onrender.com. Open it a few minutes before judging so the free server is awake.
- [ ] **Walkthrough video:** the latest version (v1.6), with voiceover.
- [ ] **Source code:** https://github.com/oshealth31/ceda-innovation-management-portal, or a ZIP of it (about 1 MB, without the `.git` folder).

## Supporting documents

- [ ] **Proposal**, 10 pages or less, exported to PDF. It covers motivation, design, stack, research and lessons learnt. Check the page count after exporting.
- [ ] **User manual**, exported to PDF.
- [ ] **Installers:** `scripts/install.sh` (Mac/Linux) and `scripts/install.ps1` (Windows), explained in `docs/INSTALL.md`.
- [ ] **README.md:** in the repository.
- [ ] **Hosting plan:** `docs/HOSTING.md`, covering provider, region, costs and 24/7 availability.
- [ ] **CVs:** one for every team member.
- [ ] **Copies of IDs (Omang):** for every team member.
- [ ] **Company documents:** the partner company's CIPA certificate and shareholder extract, if the form asks for them.

## Before you press submit

- [ ] Email hackathon@bih.co.bw about the code written before the event and about AI tools, and follow their answer.
- [ ] Sign the MoU with the partner company. CEDA owns all hackathon work (clause 5).
- [ ] Every team member is a citizen aged 18 to 35, and everyone can present on 3 December.
- [ ] Demo logins for the judges are written in the submission form.
- [ ] The total size is under 150 MB.
- [ ] Submit by midday on 23 November, not at 16:55.
